Overview / 01

A commercial-grade successor to GridLAB-D, now stewarded in the open.

Arras Energy began as GridLAB-D, developed for the U.S. Department of Energy by Pacific Northwest National Laboratory to study grid modernization. In 2018, the California Energy Commission funded SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory to harden it into HiPAS GridLAB-D — a commercial-grade simulation engine. LF Energy adopted the project in 2022 and renamed it Arras Energy, giving it a neutral, vendor-independent home for continued development.

History / 03

Two decades from research prototype to LF Energy project.

2002
PDSS demonstrated at PNNL
An agent-based solver technology demonstration lays the groundwork for the engine.
2007–2008
GridLAB-D funded and released
DOE funds development based on PDSS; the first free, open-source release ships in 2008.
2018
California Energy Commission funds HiPAS GridLAB-D
SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory builds a commercial-grade version under CEC grant EPC-17-046.
2022
LF Energy adopts the project as Arras Energy
HiPAS GridLAB-D moves to a neutral open-source foundation under the name Arras Energy.
2025
v4.3.1 "chiba-1"
A major release adds hosting-capacity, resilience, tariff, and electrification support.

For Claude users / 05

Load Arras Energy skills into Claude.

The arras-energy/claude repository holds skills: folders of markdown instructions, reference tables, and starting templates. Users add them to Claude Code or claude.ai, and the instructions are loaded into the text input when a request matches a skill's description.

  • arras-energy — installing, running, and finding your way around the repositories
  • gridlabd-glm — writing and debugging .glm models
  • gridlabd-data — weather, feeders, libraries, converters, and tools
  • arras-development — building, testing, extending, and contributing

Output is generated text: users review models and run them in Arras Energy before relying on results.

Try it
git clone https://github.com/arras-energy/claude ~/arras-claude
mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills
ln -s ~/arras-claude/skills/* ~/.claude/skills/
Then enter a text input such as
Write a GLM model of a 4.16 kV feeder with 20 houses and a recorder on the substation meter.

The request matches gridlabd-glm, so its syntax reference and feeder template are loaded and Claude generates a draft .glm file to run with gridlabd.
